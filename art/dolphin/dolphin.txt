# Format: "sprite NAME" then one row per line, '#' a black pixel and '.'
# a transparent one; a blank line ends the sprite. Every row of a sprite
# has the same width. Overlays are drawn at a position relative to the
# body (helpers/rg_mascot.h names the anchors); only black pixels are
# drawn, so an overlay never erases the body under it.
