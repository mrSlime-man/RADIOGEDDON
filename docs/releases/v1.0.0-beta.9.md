## What's new

Beta 9 turns the **Full edition** (RogueMaster, Momentum, Unleashed) into a
standalone research lab. Everything runs on the Flipper: no computer, phone,
network or cloud service is involved, and nothing in this release transmits.
The full list is in the
[CHANGELOG](https://github.com/mrSlime-man/RADIOGEDDON/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

- **Find Similar Signals** — every RAW recording gets a 64-byte *signal
  fingerprint* (frequency, modulation, timing ratios, pulse and gap
  histograms, preamble, sync, frame structure, repeats, bit length, encoding
  and pattern stability). *Find Similar...* ranks your recordings against one
  and explains each match ("Why similar?": what agrees, what contradicts).
  Scores are capped when the evidence is thin, so a short or noisy capture
  never reads as a confident match. The fingerprints are kept in
  `fingerprints.idx`, brought up to date by each search, and rebuilt
  when damaged.
- **Pulse Studio** — one RAW capture on one screen: the waveform with zoom,
  pan, a cursor and a measuring marker; preamble, glitch and frame-end marks;
  the bit each symbol decodes to (`?` where the analyzer is unsure); and Frame,
  Bits, Hex, Diff and Timing (pulse/gap histogram) pages. Long recordings are
  read one frame at a time, never loaded whole.
- **Smart Protocol Discovery** (Unknown Protocol Analysis, improved) —
  several competing readings with their evidence and scores instead of one
  answer, a bit map from each pulse to its bit, better PWM/PPM separation on
  long-gap signals, and checksum hypotheses that require independent frames.
  On the firmware's own 50 RAW test captures the encoding family is now
  right for 48 (was 43, with 3 unidentified) and the frame length for 42 (was
  35); a wrong answer now scores 67 on average against 87 for a right one
  (was 84). No protocol answer is hardcoded.
- **Adaptive Scanner** — *Standard* (the classic round robin), *Balanced
  Adaptive*, *Activity Focused* and *Custom* (your favorites first). Busy
  frequencies are visited more, but every frequency keeps a
  bounded wait; the screen shows each frequency's share of the visits and the
  longest wait. In the host simulation (3,168 bursts on 19 frequencies) it
  catches 1,031 (Balanced) and 1,129 (Focused) against 828 for Standard —
  a simulation, not a field measurement.
- **RF Activity Logger** — a long passive observation (15 min to 8 h, or
  until stopped) of the Scanner's frequency list. Rows per interval to
  `activity/*.csv`, keeping "quiet" (listened, nothing seen) apart from
  "unobserved" (nobody listened); size-limited parts; a summary with the
  busiest frequencies, first and last activity and coverage. It stops rather
  than overwrite anything.
- **Research Journal and Research XP** (optional) — a record of real events
  only (recordings, analyses, searches, comparisons, sessions, reports, logs,
  identified protocols), 12 achievements and levels. XP never rewards
  transmitting, and repeats count only up to a cap.
- **Dolphin Assistant** (optional) — one short, rule-based tip at a time
  on the dashboard ("Find similar signals?", "Few frames: record more
  samples?"), never while recording, rate-limited, dismissable (long OK) and
  switchable off. It suggests; it never acts on its own.
- **Dolphin customization and Room 2.0** — headphones, goggles, antenna,
  tool, expression and accessory; wall decorations unlocked by research
  level; *Animation level* Off / Minimal / Normal / Full. The lab room has
  six stations: Workbench (the recording you are working on), Oscilloscope,
  Radar, Research Terminal (your journal), Equipment Rack (opens the real
  tools) and the Dolphin Corner.
- **One workflow** — the app remembers the recording you are working on, and
  Analyze, Find Similar, Pulse Studio, Compare and Sessions start from it.

The **Catalog edition** (Official firmware) keeps its toolkit and Classic
menus; it gains the analyzer improvements (checksum hypotheses stay in the
Full edition). Transmit rules are
unchanged: the Catalog edition checks the firmware's region before every
transmission, and the Full edition relies on the firmware's own check.
Nothing in this release transmits on its own, predicts or decrypts codes,
recovers keys or brute-forces anything.

## Which file do I download?

| Firmware | Edition | File | API |
|----------|---------|------|-----|
| Official (flipperzero.one) 1.4.x | Catalog | `radiogeddon-catalog-official.fap` | 87.1 |
| RogueMaster | Full | `radiogeddon-full-roguemaster.fap` | 88.16 |
| Momentum mntm-012 | Full | `radiogeddon-full-momentum.fap` | 87.1 |
| Unleashed | Full | `radiogeddon-full-unleashed.fap` | 88.9 |

Copy it to `SD Card/apps/Sub-GHz/` (replacing beta 8) and open **Apps →
Sub-GHz → RadioGeddon** (Catalog) or **RadioGeddon Full**. The Full edition's
ten modules are inside the `.fap`; the firmware unpacks them when the app
starts. Settings, recordings and sessions from beta 8 keep working. Full
steps:
[Installation](https://github.com/mrSlime-man/RADIOGEDDON/blob/main/docs/INSTALLATION.md).

> [!NOTE]
> Momentum and Official both report API 87.1, so the Flipper's version check
> does not tell the two files apart: install the one named for your firmware.

## Memory

Measured from the built files (`scripts/fap_size.py`: the bytes the loader
places in RAM), not on a device:

| | Beta 8 | Beta 9 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Catalog edition, resident | 77,782 B | 80,553 B |
| Full edition, resident | 119,428 B | 119,132 B |
| Largest Full module loaded at once | 16,380 B (Sessions) | 21,904 B (Pulse Studio) |
| Full modules | 6 | 10 |

The Full edition's resident code is slightly *smaller* than beta 8 although
it does much more: every new tool, and beta 8's reports and Pulse Timeline,
are modules loaded only while their screen is open, and settings are
table-driven. The Catalog edition grew by 2.7 KB (analyzer improvements).
While Pulse Studio is open, its module and its screen (a few KB) are in
memory on top of the resident code; it refuses to open (and says so) when that does
not fit.

## Build & verification status

- Built from one source tree as two editions for four firmware families, each
  against its own SHA-256-pinned SDK (RogueMaster from its pinned source
  tree). For every artifact the API version, the manifest and every imported
  symbol are verified against that SDK; for the Full builds the same holds
  for each of the ten embedded modules.
- 2,528 host unit-test checks under AddressSanitizer and UBSan (was 2,115),
  new suites for fingerprints (73), the adaptive scheduler, the activity log
  (29), the journal and assistant (90) and Pulse Studio (106). Also: 661
  format checks (with beta 8 settings files), 289 decoder and preset checks,
  406 analyzer checks on the firmware's captures (was 354), 43 engine
  lifecycle checks (adaptive scanning added), 47 fingerprint-index and 20
  activity-log file checks through the firmware's own FlipperFormat and a
  stub SD card, and 407 offline UI checks that draw Pulse Studio on every
  page and frame, the adaptive Scanner, the six room stations and every
  dolphin look with the firmware's fonts on a host canvas. libFuzzer runs on
  the RAW reader, analyzer, Database, session files, UI text and, new, the
  fingerprint index and journal files. GCC `-fanalyzer` and clang-tidy report
  no findings.
- The previews below and in the README are **synthetic renderings** from the
  host canvas, not screenshots of a Flipper.
- Hardware: not verified for anything new since beta 2. The checks to run are
  in [HARDWARE_CHECKLIST.md](https://github.com/mrSlime-man/RADIOGEDDON/blob/main/docs/HARDWARE_CHECKLIST.md)
  and [VERIFICATION.md](https://github.com/mrSlime-man/RADIOGEDDON/blob/main/docs/VERIFICATION.md).

## Verifying these downloads

`SHA256SUMS` is attached. Check it with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS` (Linux) or the
per-file commands in the installation guide. Each `.fap` also has a signed
build-provenance attestation, checked by the release workflow before upload,
linking it to this release's workflow run and commit:

```bash
gh attestation verify radiogeddon-full-momentum.fap --repo mrSlime-man/RADIOGEDDON
```

`BUILD_INFO.txt` records the edition, app id, SDK, API, source revision and
embedded modules each artifact was built from.

## Known limitations

- Nothing new since beta 2 is verified on a device yet: in particular the
  fingerprint index on a large SD card, an 8-hour activity log on battery,
  Pulse Studio's memory headroom, and how the new screens read on the real
  display.
- Similarity is evidence, not identity: two recordings of different devices
  of the same model can look alike, and one device can look different on a
  noisy day. "Why similar?" says what the score rests on.
- The Adaptive Scanner's gains come from a host simulation; a real band's
  traffic may differ. Standard mode is unchanged from beta 8.
- The activity logger observes one frequency at a time (the CC1101 is a
  single receiver); "unobserved" in its log means exactly that.
- The lab room's radar is an illustration; the workbench, oscilloscope and
  terminal show real data.
- The Catalog edition is not in the Apps Catalog yet: the submission needs
  qFlipper screenshots from hardware, and it has not been submitted.
- RadioGeddon does not recover keys, decrypt payloads, predict rolling codes
  or brute-force anything, and refuses to replay decoded rolling-code
  protocols.
